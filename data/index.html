    Device

    LD2450 Radar

    Presence stays active for the occupancy timeout after the last target was lost. A target counts as moving while its speed is at least the moving threshold (0 = always moving). No hardware driver yet: with simulation disabled the radar delivers no data. Pins take effect after a restart.

    Zones

    Sensor at the top, looking down. Drag a corner to resize a zone or object, drag its body to move it. Grid: 1 m.

    A zone appears in Home Assistant only once it is enabled. Min. signal quality ignores targets with a weaker signal in that zone: a higher value means less sensitive, 0 turns the filter off.

    Objects

    Furniture and doors for orientation only, not reported to Home Assistant. The arrow shows the facing direction.

    Illuminance (BH1750)

    No hardware driver yet: with simulation disabled the sensor delivers no data. Pins take effect after a restart.

    MQTT / Home Assistant

    How often the firmware sends the MQTT server a sign of life. Lower = connection losses are detected sooner, higher = more tolerant of short Wi-Fi dropouts.

    How long the firmware waits for a reply from the server before it considers the connection lost. Must be lower than the keepalive.

    Home Assistant Entities

    Disabled entities are removed from Home Assistant. Zone occupancy entities follow the zone's own enabled switch. "Still" is occupancy on with motion off.

    Wi-Fi Connection Stability

    Some Wi-Fi chips briefly power down to save energy, which delays replies and can cause "Connection lost" messages. Disabling power saving fixes this at the cost of a slightly higher power draw – negligible for a permanently powered device.

    Update einspielen

    Während des Updates und des anschließenden Neustarts ist das Gerät nicht erreichbar (ca. 1 Minute). Diese Seite geöffnet lassen.

    Firmware (firmware.bin)
    Dateisystem-Image (littlefs.bin, optional)

    Nur Firmware: Einstellungen bleiben erhalten, die Web-Oberfläche bleibt aber auf dem alten Stand. Dateisystem-Image: ersetzt die Web-Oberfläche (index.html, app.js, style.css) und löscht dabei die gespeicherten Einstellungen – sie werden nach dem Neustart automatisch aus dem Backup wiederhergestellt. Beides zusammen = vollständiges Release-Update.

      Backup wiederherstellen

      Vor jedem Update lädt der Browser automatisch ein Konfigurations-Backup herunter (presence-config-backup-<Datum_Uhrzeit>.json, im Download-Ordner des Browsers). Genau diese Datei lässt sich hier wieder einspielen (/api/config/restore) – sie ersetzt alle aktuellen Einstellungen.
